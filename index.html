Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about loan applications and processes.

1. What documents are required for a loan?
Generally, you need basic identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, last 3-6 months bank statements, and income proof (salary slips or ITR returns).
2. How long does the loan process take?
Initial consultation and preliminary eligibility check take 24 to 48 hours upon submission of basic mandatory documents.
3. How much loan amount can I get?
Loan amounts depend on your monthly net income, existing EMI obligations, credit profile, and the value of collateral (for secured loans).
4. What is the minimum income requirement?
Minimum income requirements typically start from ₹20,000/month for personal loans, though exact criteria vary depending on loan type and city.
5. Can self-employed individuals apply?
Yes! Self-employed business owners, doctors, consultants, and professionals can apply with GST registration, business continuity proof, and ITR.
